ABOUT OUR SANCTUARY

Discover the Coastal Charm of Mansalay, Oriental Mindoro

Nestled along the pristine coastline of Sitio Ligaya, Barangay San Pedro in Mansalay, Oriental Mindoro (formerly recognized as Aron Beach Resort), Sky & Shore Beach Resort stands as a premier eco-coastal haven designed to connect travelers with the raw, breathtaking beauty of Southern Mindoro.

Our Mission

To provide sustainable, authentic, and heartwarming Filipino hospitality that celebrates coastal wellness, preserves local marine wonders, and supports the local community of Mansalay.

Our Vision

To be Oriental Mindoro's benchmark coastal destination known for unforgettable family holidays, inspiring retreats, team-building gatherings, and eco-conscious leisure.

Resort Competitive Advantage:
  • Dual Landscape Wonder: Features both a turquoise shoreline and a panoramic Mountain Park with 126 scenic steps overlooking the Sibuyan Sea.
  • All-Inclusive Hospitality: Thoughtfully curated tour packages inclusive of aircon rooms, full-board meals, island excursions, and nightly bonfires.
  • Community-Focused Tourism: Supporting local boat operators, fishermen, and cultural appreciation of Mansalay's Mangyan heritage.

LOCATION & ACCESS

Getting to Mansalay, Oriental Mindoro

Your travel guide to reaching our coastal sanctuary safely and seamlessly.

How to Get There
1. From Manila / Batangas Port:

Take a FastCat / RORO ferry from Batangas Port to Calapan Port or Dangay Port (Roxas, Oriental Mindoro). From Roxas port, Mansalay is merely 25-30 minutes away via van or tricycle.

2. From Calapan City:

Board a Southbound bus (ALPS, RORO Bus) or passenger van bound for San Jose/Bulalacao/Mansalay along the Western Nautical Highway (approx. 2.5 to 3 hours scenic drive).

3. Local Landmark & Arrival:

Alight at Mansalay Town Proper or Sitio Ligaya, Brgy. San Pedro. Tricycles directly service Sky & Shore Beach Resort (formerly Aron Beach Resort). Private vehicles enjoy direct concrete road access and free secured parking.

TOURIST GUIDE

Essential Travel Tips & Mindoro Reminders

Ensure a smooth, safe, and respectful holiday experience in Southern Oriental Mindoro.

Best Time to Visit & Weather

The dry and sunny summer season from November to May is the ideal window for calm seas, snorkeling clarity, and beach lounging. Mansalay enjoys warm tropical climates with cool night sea breezes.

What to Bring & Dress Code

Bring reef-safe sunscreen, comfortable swimwear, aqua shoes (for pebble-sand exploration), insect repellent, waterproof dry bags for boat hopping, personal toiletries, and comfortable trekking shoes for the 126 steps mountain park.

Eco Guidelines & Custom

Mansalay prides itself on pristine nature. Practice "Leave No Trace": do not collect live seashells or corals. Mansalay is home to indigenous Hanunuo Mangyan communities; treat local traditions, handicrafts, and cultural heritage with high respect.

Currency & Budget Planning

Mansalay town has ATM kiosks and GCash cash-in centers, but we recommend carrying cash in Philippine Pesos (PHP) for municipal fees (₱50/pax environmental fee), boat tips, and fresh fish market purchases.
